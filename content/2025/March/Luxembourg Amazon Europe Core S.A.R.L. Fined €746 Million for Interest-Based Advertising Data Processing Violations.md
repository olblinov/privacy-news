# Summary

The Administrative Tribunal of Luxembourg has confirmed a substantial fine of **€746,000,000** against Amazon Europe Core S.A.R.L. for multiple violations of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) concerning its processing of personal data for interest-based advertising. The tribunal, on March 18, 2025, rejected Amazon's appeal against the initial decision issued by the Commission Nationale de la Protection des Données (CNPD) on July 15, 2021, upholding both the administrative fine and the requirement for corrective measures, under threat of a daily penalty. Although the decision confirms the violations and sanctions, its effects are suspended pending a possible further appeal to the Administrative Court.

# Details

## Background

The case originated from a decision made by the Commission Nationale de la Protection des Données (CNPD) on **July 15, 2021**, concerning Amazon Europe Core S.A.R.L. for violations of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Amazon appealed this decision on **October 29, 2021**. An administrative hearing took place on **January 9, 2024**, before the Administrative Tribunal of Luxembourg. On **March 18, 2025**, the Administrative Tribunal of Luxembourg issued a judgment rejecting Amazon's appeal and confirming the CNPD's initial decision. This means the imposed fine for GDPR infringements and the ordered corrective measures are upheld.

## Legal Analysis

The Administrative Tribunal validated the CNPD's analysis, confirming that Amazon Europe Core S.A.R.L. violated several articles of the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) in its processing of personal data for **interest-based advertising**. Specifically, the company was found to have violated **Articles 6, 12 to 17, and 21** of the GDPR.

The violations include:

- **Non-compliance with the lawful basis for processing personal data**.
- **Non-compliance with transparency obligations**.
- **Failure to provide information to the individuals concerned** by the processing of their personal data.
- **Violation of the right to access** processed data.
- **Violation of the rights to rectification and erasure** of personal data.
- **Violation of the right to object** to the processing of personal data.

Furthermore, the tribunal observed that Amazon Europe Core S.A.R.L. had **failed to adopt the necessary corrective measures** to comply with Articles 6, 12 to 17, and 21 of the GDPR.

## Sanction

The Administrative Tribunal confirmed the administrative fine of **€746,000,000** imposed on Amazon Europe Core S.A.R.L.. In addition to the fine, the company is required to implement **corrective measures**. A daily penalty (astreinte journalière) of **€746,000** will be applied if Amazon fails to comply with these corrective measures. The initial CNPD decision also ordered the **publication of the decision**.

However, the effects of the CNPD's decision remain **suspended** during the appeal period and, if applicable, during any potential appeal proceedings before the Administrative Court. The judgment by the Administrative Tribunal, dated March 18, 2025, can be appealed before the Administrative Court. Despite the public nature of the Tribunal's decision, national data protection law obliges the CNPD to respect professional secrecy (Article 42) and prevents it from commenting on individual cases.
