# Summary

The **Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP)** concluded an investigation in December 2024, imposing an administrative fine of **24,854.50 RON (equivalent to 5,000 EUR)** on **RED&WHITE 2022 MANAGEMENT S.A.**. The sanction was issued for the operator's **violation of Article 28, paragraph (3), letter a) of Regulation (UE) 2016/679 (GDPR)**. This infringement arose from the operator's failure to provide **documented instructions to its data processor** regarding the specific **categories of data subjects** to whom an email about a football team's financing campaign was transmitted. The database used for this campaign contained personal data, including names and email addresses, of both club supporters and other individuals.

# Details

## Background

The investigation by the Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP) into **RED&WHITE 2022 MANAGEMENT S.A.** was initiated in December 2024. This probe followed a notification to the Authority from the operator itself, or its appointed processor, concerning a **possible violation of Regulation (UE) 2016/679 (GDPR)**. The incident occurred within the context of a **crowdfunding campaign** aimed at securing financial support for a football team. As the majority shareholder of the football team, the operator sent an email promoting this financing campaign to a **large database of individuals** who had previously purchased tickets for the team's matches. The email was distributed through an "empowered person" or data processor, and the database used included personal data such as names, surnames, and email addresses, belonging to both club supporters and other individuals.

## Legal Analysis

During its investigation, the ANSPDCP determined that **RED&WHITE 2022 MANAGEMENT S.A.** had breached its obligations under the GDPR. The core finding was that the operator **failed to provide documented instructions to its data processor** regarding the **specific categories of data subjects** within the utilized database to whom the financing campaign email should have been sent. This failure was deemed a direct **violation of Article 28, paragraph (3), letter a) of Regulation (UE) 2016/679 (GDPR)**. The cited Article 28(3) mandates that any processing carried out by a data processor on behalf of a data controller must be governed by a contract or another legal act that, among other requirements, explicitly defines the **"categories of data subjects"**. Furthermore, this article also stipulates that the data processor is only permitted to process personal data **"on the basis of documented instructions from the operator"** (controller). The absence of such clear, documented guidance from **RED&WHITE 2022 MANAGEMENT S.A.** to its processor concerning the target audience for the email campaign formed the basis of the infringement.

## Sanction

As a direct consequence of the identified breach of **Article 28, paragraph (3), letter a) of the GDPR**, the Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP) imposed an **administrative financial penalty** on **RED&WHITE 2022 MANAGEMENT S.A.**. The fine levied amounted to **24,854.50 Romanian Lei (RON)**, which is equivalent to **5,000 Euros**. This sanction was applied due to the operator's failure to provide its data processor with documented instructions regarding the categories of data subjects involved in the email campaign, an action explicitly required by GDPR provisions.
