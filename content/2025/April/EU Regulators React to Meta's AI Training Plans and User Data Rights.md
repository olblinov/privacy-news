# Meta's AI Training Initiative

## Data Usage and Scope

Meta plans to **begin training its artificial intelligence (AI) systems with personal data from European Facebook and Instagram users by the end of May 2025**, with some sources specifying May 27, 2025. This initiative aims to **develop and improve Meta's generative AI services**, including the Meta AI chatbot on WhatsApp and language models like Llama.

The data Meta intends to use includes **all publicly visible information from adult users' profiles**, such as posts, photos, comments, videos, and captions. This encompasses **both historical and future content** that has been made public. Importantly, Meta states that only content published by users over 18 years old will be used for the core AI model training.

In addition to publicly posted content, Meta will also use **data from user interactions with its AI services**, such as information entered into AI chatbots or image generation tools, regardless of the user's age. There is **conflicting information** regarding the use of WhatsApp data for training; one source indicates that messages where Meta AI is involved are used for training and objection is not possible, while another states that data of Meta AI users on WhatsApp are not concerned.

## Legal Basis and Related Concerns

Meta is relying on **"legitimate interests" as the legal basis** for processing personal data for AI training, rather than user consent. This approach has raised **significant concerns among European data protection authorities (DPAs)**.

Key concerns identified by various authorities include:

- **Lawful Basis and Transparency**: Critics question whether Meta's reliance on "legitimate interests" overrides users' fundamental rights and freedoms, especially when personal data is reused in ways users would not reasonably expect, such as AI training. There is also concern about whether individuals are clearly and fully informed about this broad data usage, particularly that old posts or images may be used without their active consent.
- **Purpose Limitation and Data Minimization**: Repurposing data originally shared for social interaction to train AI models is seen as a substantial shift that may not be compatible with the original purposes of collection. The broad, unrestricted use of all public content, regardless of its relevance or sensitivity, also raises questions about compliance with data minimization principles.
- **Irreversibility and the Right to Erasure**: Once data is used to train AI models, it is considered **irrevocably embedded** into those models. This technical limitation means data cannot typically be "unlearned" or extracted from an AI system, which may conflict with the GDPR's right to erasure or "the right to be forgotten" and fundamentally undermines users’ ability to exercise this data protection right.

# User Rights and Objection Mechanism

## The Right to Object

Under Article 21 of the GDPR, users have the **right to object to Meta's processing of their personal data** for AI training, given Meta's stated reliance on "legitimate interest". Meta has implemented an **opt-out mechanism** to allow users to exercise this right.

## How to Object and Deadlines

Users who do not want their data used for AI training **must actively object**. Meta has begun informing users about these plans via email and in-app notifications, providing links to objection forms.

Objection forms are available online for:

- **Facebook users**: https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/712876720715583.
- **Instagram users**: https://help.instagram.com/contact/767264225370182.
- **Individuals not using Meta products (without login)**: https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/510058597920541. This form may require more precise details and evidence.

Users do not need to provide a justification for their objection. A confirmation will be received via a pop-up in the app or by email, validating the objection. It is also possible to protest by letter.

To prevent past personal data from being used, **objection must be exercised before the end of May 2025**, with some sources specifically stating May 27, 2025, as the start date for training. If an objection is submitted after this deadline, it will only apply to future data use and subsequent training cycles, and **will not retroactively remove data already used for training** due to the technical irreversibility of AI models.

## Scope and Effectiveness of Objection

An objection primarily applies to **content that the user has personally published publicly**. It does **not apply to photos or posts about the user that have been published by other accounts**. Whether such content is used depends on whether the publishing user has objected.

For users with **multiple Facebook and Instagram accounts**, a single objection may apply to all accounts if they are linked within the same "Account Center". Otherwise, separate objections are required for each account. **Prior objections made in 2024 are still valid** if confirmed by Meta.

Users who object to AI training can still use Meta's AI chatbots on WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram. However, their **interactions with these chatbots will still be used for AI training**, and there is no specified mechanism to object to this specific use.

## Special Considerations for Minors

While data published by **underage users is, by default, excluded from Meta's AI training**, it is possible that data concerning minors (users or non-users) may appear in content published by adult users. In such cases, **minors or those exercising parental responsibility are encouraged to consider exercising the right to object using the form for non-users of Meta services**. Data protection authorities have also specifically questioned Meta on its handling of images of minors posted by adults.

# Regulatory Scrutiny and Collaboration

## European Data Protection Authorities' Stance

European data protection authorities, including the AP (Netherlands), Garante Privacy (Italy), IDPC (Ireland), CNIL (France), UODO (Poland), HmbBfDI (Germany), and Datatilsynet (Norway), are **closely monitoring and collaborating to assess the compliance of Meta's AI training plans with GDPR**. The Irish Data Protection Commission (IDPC) is the leading authority given Meta's European headquarters are located in Ireland.

## Previous Delays and Ongoing Discussions

Meta's plans to train AI with European user data were **previously postponed in the summer of 2024** following interventions and discussions with the Irish DPC, specifically concerning the legal basis and transparency of such data processing. Although Meta has since implemented a revised approach with an opt-out mechanism, **discussions are still ongoing** between the DPC and Meta, and these could lead to further developments in data protection. Authorities are paying particular attention to the **lawfulness of these practices, the actual effectiveness of the right to object, and the compatibility between the original purposes of data processing and this new use of data**.
