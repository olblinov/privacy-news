# Summary

The Italian Supervisory Authority (Garante) has imposed an **administrative fine of €5 million on Luka Inc.**, the US company behind the generative AI chatbot "Replika". This decision, stemming from an investigation initiated by the Garante in February 2023, confirmed that Luka Inc. failed to identify a proper legal basis for its data processing operations, provided an inadequate and misleading privacy policy, and lacked effective age verification mechanisms, thereby posing significant risks to users, especially minors. In addition to the fine, the Garante ordered the company to bring its processing operations and age verification system into compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

# Details

## Background

The proceedings originated from an investigation initiated by the Italian Supervisory Authority (Garante) of its own motion, following press reports and preliminary fact-finding concerning the Replika service. Replika is described as a chatbot with a written and voice interface, developed and managed by the US company Luka Inc., and based on a generative AI system. It allows users to create a "virtual companion" that can act as a confidant, therapist, romantic partner, or mentor. The chatbot utilizes a Large Language Model (LLM) that continuously improves through user interaction.

In February 2023, the Garante ordered the temporary blocking of the Replika application due to concerns that Luka's data processing practices could violate personal data protection laws and pose concrete risks to minors. Specific issues identified included the absence of a clear legal basis for data processing, an inadequate privacy policy, and a lack of mechanisms for verifying users' ages. Although Luka stated that minors were excluded from potential users, no age verification was implemented at registration or during service use until February 2, 2023. #minors 

Following the initial urgent measure, Luka blocked access to Replika from Italy and outlined a series of initiatives to comply with the Garante's requests, such as implementing more robust age verification mechanisms, improving content moderation, and updating its privacy policy. The temporary limitation was suspended in June 2023, on the condition that Luka would adopt specific compliance measures, including presenting an updated privacy policy, implementing an age-gate mechanism and a "cooling-off period" to prevent minors from easily re-registering, facilitating data subject rights, and submitting plans for preventing minor access and moderating inappropriate content.

## Legal Analysis

The Garante asserted its competence based on the **"targeting" criterion** of Article 3(2) of the GDPR, as the Replika service was offered to individuals in the European Union (specifically Italy). Luka Inc.'s claim of having an EU establishment in the Netherlands to invoke the "one-stop-shop" mechanism was not supported by any evidence.

The investigation confirmed several infringements of the GDPR by Luka Inc. as of February 2, 2023:

- **Failure to identify a legal basis for data processing (Articles 5.1(a) and 6 GDPR)**: Luka Inc.'s privacy policy at the time did not granularly specify the legal basis for various data processing operations, including data used for the development of its Large Language Model (LLM). Generic references to contract performance, consent, or legal authorization were deemed insufficient to meet the principle of lawfulness. 
- **Inadequate privacy policy and transparency obligations (Articles 5.1(a), 12, and 13 GDPR)** #transparency :
    - The privacy policy was only available in English, not in Italian, which is the language of the country where the service was offered.
    - It lacked detailed information regarding the purposes of distinct processing activities (e.g., "Chatbot Interaction" vs. "Model Development").
    - It did not explicitly state that the service was exclusively for adults, only mentioning users under 13. #minors 
    - It failed to provide precise information on personal data retention periods or the criteria used to determine them. #retention 
    - It contained misleading information about potential data transfers to the USA, which the company itself later contradicted. #transfers 
    - It included information on Article 22 GDPR rights (automated decision-making) that implied such processing was occurring, which the company later denied, leading to user confusion and violating transparency principles. #rights  #change 
- **Lack of adequate age verification mechanisms (Articles 5.1(c), 24, and 25.1 GDPR)**: #minors  #pbd  Before the Garante's intervention, Luka did not implement any age verification procedures at registration or during service use, despite claiming to exclude minors. This exposed minors, potentially under 13, to inappropriate content (including sexually explicit material) and significant risks due to the sensitive nature of chatbot conversations. Technical assessments revealed that even the currently implemented age verification system remains deficient, allowing users to bypass checks (e.g., changing birth dates post-profile creation, bypassing cooling-off periods with incognito browsing, and a lack of robust language analysis for age signals #profiling ). The Garante found that Luka failed to implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure data minimization and privacy by design/default.

## Sanction

The Italian Supervisory Authority imposed an **administrative fine of €5 million** on Luka Inc.. This amount was determined by considering the multiple, interconnected violations as a single instance, with the fine capped at the most severe infringement, which was identified as the transparency violations.

The Garante's calculation factored in several elements: #enforcement_pattern 

- **High Gravity of Violations**: This was due to the infringements concerning fundamental principles like accountability and transparency, the failure to identify legal bases before processing, the processing of data beyond necessity (especially minors' data), the high risks associated with innovative and disruptive AI technology, the global cross-border scope of the processing, and the large number of potentially affected Italian users (estimated over 10 million global users). The duration of the infringement, since the app's release in November 2017 until the Garante's urgent intervention, also contributed to the high severity.
- **Negligent Intent**: While the violations were considered unintentional, they were deemed to be due to serious negligence, as Luka Inc. should have been aware of and applied the GDPR, especially given the scale and innovative nature of its service.
- **Aggravating Factors**: These included Luka's high degree of responsibility for failing to adopt appropriate risk mitigation measures at the service's launch and its limited cooperation with the Garante (e.g., not submitting defense statements after the infringement notice).
- **Mitigating Factors**: Partial credit was given for the subsequent updates to the privacy policy and the implementation of age-gate mechanisms, although these were not deemed fully exhaustive in addressing the issues.

The **€5 million fine breakdown** was set as:

- **€3,000,000** for the transparency and data subject rights violations (Articles 5.1(a), 12, and 13), being the most serious. #transparency 
- **+€1,000,000** for the lawfulness and legal basis violations (Articles 5.1(a) and 6).
- **+€1,000,000** for the data minimization, privacy by design/default, and age verification violations (Articles 5.1(c) and 25.1). #pbd #minors 

In addition to the fine, the Garante issued a **compliance order**, requiring Luka Inc. to rectify remaining deficiencies:

- **Privacy Policy**: The company must ensure its privacy policy (current version from February 2024) is available in Italian, provides specific data retention periods or criteria, and clarifies information regarding data transfers to avoid misleading users about transfers to the USA.
- **Age Verification System**: Luka must address ongoing shortcomings in its age verification system, such as allowing users to easily change birth dates post-profile creation, preventing bypasses via incognito browsing, and implementing more robust language analysis mechanisms to identify underage users. Luka Inc. is mandated to communicate the initiatives undertaken to comply with these corrective measures to the Garante within sixty days.

An **ancillary penalty** of publishing the decision on the Garante's website was also ordered, given the sensitivity of the data, the large scale of processing involving a high number of individuals, and the inherent risks associated with innovative AI technology without proper safeguards. The Garante also stated its intention to conduct a **separate and autonomous investigation** to further assess the lawfulness of processing operations related to the entire lifecycle of the generative AI system, specifically focusing on the legal bases for training the AI model.
