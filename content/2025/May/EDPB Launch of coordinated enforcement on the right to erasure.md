# Introduction to CEF 2025

The European Data Protection Board (EDPB) launched its Coordinated Enforcement Framework (CEF) action for 2025 on **March 5, 2025**. This initiative shifts its focus to the implementation of the **right to erasure**, also known as the “right to be forgotten” (Art. 17 GDPR). This follows a year-long coordinated action in 2024 that focused on the right of access.

# Rationale and Participants

This topic was selected by the Board during its October 2024 plenary because the right to erasure is **one of the most frequently exercised GDPR rights**, and Data Protection Authorities (DPAs) **frequently receive complaints** from individuals concerning it.

For this initiative, **30 Data Protection Authorities (DPAs)** across Europe will participate, along with **the European Data Protection Supervisor (EDPS)**.

# Enforcement Process and Objectives

During 2025, participating DPAs will contact various controllers across Europe. This contact may involve **opening new formal investigations** or conducting **fact-finding exercises**. Depending on the findings, additional follow-up actions may be undertaken.

DPAs will specifically check **how controllers handle and respond to requests for erasure** and, in particular, **how they apply the conditions and exceptions** for exercising this right. Throughout the year, DPAs will maintain close contact to share and discuss their findings. The results from these national actions will be aggregated and analyzed to provide **deeper insight** into the topic, enabling **targeted follow-ups** at both national and EU levels.

# Background of the CEF

The Coordinated Enforcement Framework (CEF) is highlighted as a **key action of the EDPB** under its **2024-2027 strategy**. Its primary aim is to **streamline enforcement and cooperation** among DPAs.

In the three years prior to 2025, three other CEF actions were carried out on different topics:

- The use of cloud-based services by the public sector.
- The designation and position of Data Protection Officers.
- The implementation of the right of access by controllers.

Many specific DPAs have publicly announced their participation in the CEF 2025, including those from Austria (AT DPA), Bulgaria (BG DPA), Czechia (CS DPA), Denmark (DA DPA), various German DPAs (Baden-Wuerttemberg, Berlin, Brandenburg), Greece (EL DPA), Spain (ES DPA), Finland (FI DPA), Croatia (HR DPA), Ireland (IE DPA), Italy (IT DPA), Liechtenstein (LI DPA), Luxembourg (LU DPA), Malta (MT DPA), and Slovenia (SI DPA).

# Latest Enforcement News (General)

The source also provides recent news regarding enforcement actions by various Supervisory Authorities (SAs):

- **August 12, 2025**: The Greek SA imposed a fine on an association for the transmission of sensitive data, failure to facilitate the right of access, and lack of cooperation with the SA.
- **July 17, 2025**: The Swedish SA issued an administrative fine against two companies in the SL Group.
- **July 15, 2025**: The Italian SA imposed fines of **420,000 EUR** on Autostrade per l’Italia spa.
- **July 15, 2025**: The Swedish SA issued an administrative fine against the Equality Ombudsman when personal data was collected via a web form.
- **July 15, 2025**: The Italian SA fined a high school regarding the use of biometrics for attendance recording.
